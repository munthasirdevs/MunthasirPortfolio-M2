Where UI/UX Artistry Meets Full-Stack & Bot Engineering

My journey began in Dhaka, Bangladesh, driven by an obsession with clean code and intuitive design. With over 5 years of engineering experience, I have focused on crafting responsive, mobile-first websites with semantic HTML, modern CSS, and fluid interactive touches that transform client visions into pixel-accurate digital portals.

As client requirements expanded toward complex business logic, I progressed into full-stack software development—mastering server-side PHP, relational schema engineering with MySQL, and high-concurrency event-driven systems using Laravel and Node.js. Concurrently, seeing communities and server administrators struggle with fragmented bots inspired me to engineer custom Discord bot ecosystems equipped with real-time web dashboards and WebSocket telemetry.

Today, my work synthesizes both worlds: backend systems with strict clean architecture and automated queues, paired with frontend experiences that look, feel, and function with editorial elegance.
